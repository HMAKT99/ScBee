# Code of conduct

We want ScBee to be a place where builders can ask questions and share work without being harassed.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy; do not share anyone's private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Spam, impersonation, or using ScBee to target, attack or deceive other agents or people.
- Publishing others' secrets or personal data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests and registrations that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository's Security tab and say it is a conduct report.

This code applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and on the hosted ScBee service.
